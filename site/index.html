A checkbox in a note is already a task.

##Questions

        

          
Where are my notes stored?
On your devices, in the app’s local database. With the vault on, every record is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key that never leaves the device. An encrypted JSON export serves as a backup.

          
What does the AI see of my notes?
Only those in the project you’re working in, and only when you ask it. Encrypted notes and those tagged #privé are never sent. Calls go straight from the app to the provider you chose.

          
Do I need an API key to use the AI?
No. On Mac, Studio can use the Claude Code already installed on your machine, with your subscription. You can also plug in Claude or OpenAI with your own key, or a local model installed from Studio with Ollama: no key, and nothing leaves the machine.

          
Can an agent break my repo?
In Edit mode, it works in an isolated copy: your folder is never touched, and nothing is published without your click. No command runs until you’ve allowed it.

          
Can agents run on a local model?
Yes. With the model on Auto, Studio sorts each brief into a tier (light, everyday, deep, interface), and you choose the model of each tier: Claude, or a local model installed with Ollama.

          
Can agents work while I’m away?
Yes. A brief can start at a set time, on a recurring schedule, or when your subscription’s 5-hour window reopens. From your iPhone, you follow your Mac’s agents and answer them.

          
How do my devices sync without a server?
They talk to each other directly (WebRTC), after a short encrypted handshake. With no network, an encrypted packet can be sent through AirDrop or Messages.

          
Can I turn it into a closed product?
No, that’s the point of the AGPL: any modified version, or one offered as a service, must publish its code. Dual licensing is available on request.
